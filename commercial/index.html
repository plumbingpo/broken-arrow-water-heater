Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Every hour without hot water costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. For a salon, it empties the chairs. In a hotel, it brings complaints at the front desk, and in an apartment building it leads to tenant disputes. Broken Arrow Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out heating elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, failed recirculation pumps, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Broken Arrow Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
